Staten Island Ferry

The Staten Island Ferry connects Lower Manhattan with St. George on Staten Island. The ride crosses New York Harbor and offers views of the Statue of Liberty and the Manhattan skyline.

A little background

About the landmark

How it began

Ferry service between Manhattan and Staten Island dates back to the 18th century. The city took over the service in 1905, and the ferry became an important part of the public transportation system.

Today

The ferry operates every day and is free to ride. Passengers can enjoy the harbor views during the trip, which usually takes about 25 minutes each way. Check the official schedule before traveling.
