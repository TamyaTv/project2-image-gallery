Yankee Stadium

Yankee Stadium is a baseball stadium in the Concourse neighborhood of the Bronx. It is home to the New York Yankees and hosts other sporting and community events.

A little background

About the landmark

How it began

The current Yankee Stadium opened in 2009, replacing the original stadium nearby. Its design includes references to the original ballpark, including a version of the historic frieze along the upper deck.

Today

The stadium hosts Yankees home games, concerts, and special events. Visitors can also find stadium tours on selected dates when the event schedule allows.
