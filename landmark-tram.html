Roosevelt Island Tramway

The Roosevelt Island Tramway connects Roosevelt Island with Manhattan, crossing above the East River. The ride offers a different view of the city and the river.

A little background

About the landmark

How it began

The tramway opened in 1976 as a way to connect the growing residential community on Roosevelt Island with Manhattan. It was the first commuter aerial tramway in North America.

Today

The tramway is part of the city’s public transportation network and accepts the same payment methods as the subway and buses. The short ride is also popular for its views of the skyline and river.
